# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful and constructive. No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or publishing others' private
information. Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules and may ban repeat
offenders. Report concerns privately through a repository security advisory or to the maintainers' contact on the
organisation profile; reports are handled in confidence.
